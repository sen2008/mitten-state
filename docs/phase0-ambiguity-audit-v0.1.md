## 2. Ambiguity list

Severity: BLOCKING = cannot code M0 without resolving. SOFT = implementer could choose without violating a decision. COSMETIC = wording/numbering.

### BLOCKING

- **B1 — County disaggregation of the macro layer.** Appendix §0 / roadmap Phase 1 item 1. The $70B budget, 7 departments, and all starting conditions are statewide; M0 simulates one region of 12 counties with county-level inputs (`roads_c`, `u_c`, populations, county-sector aggregates). No doc says how to split anything to county grain, nor the Southeast's aggregate population (needed to compute `S_SE`). Without this, items 1, 3, 4, 6 have no initial state.
- **B2 — Tax-base definition and lag mechanics.** Appendix §1. Rates, ranges, and ±1pt yields are given, but the *base* (the dollar quantity rate multiplies) is never defined or initialized, and the "50% in Q+1, 100% from Q+2, base/migration lags 4–8 quarters" rule has no functional form (step vs ramp vs distributed lag). Item 2 cannot be coded; only its UI labels can.
- **B3 — Firm profitability function.** Coupling §1.5/§1.6 + appendix (silent). Trigger bands (hire/freeze/layoff/close) are proposed, but the profit/margin function they trigger *on* — inputs (wages, taxes, infra, demand, policy) combined how, in what units — does not exist in any doc. The coupling spec itself declares this gap (§1.6, open ambiguity 1). Item 3 is fully blocked; item 4's event stream has no producer.
- **B4 — Household formation and initial conditions.** Appendix §3 + coupling §4.4. The Money rule needs households (members, earners, shared $), starting savings, payday weekday, and daily CoL-drain timing. Household size "1–4, mean ~2.4" appears only in the coupling spec; earner definition (who works: adults non-retired?), initial savings (months of CoL in reserve at spawn?), and spawn employment assignment (who starts with a job, at which firm, at what wage) are nowhere. Items 4 and 5 cannot initialize.
- **B5 — Exodus-ledger counting unit: citizen vs household.** Appendix §5 + coupling §4.2. The exodus threshold is 250,000 real-equivalent *persons*; the booking rule says "one lens household exit books `S_r`." A household of 3 is 3 persons, not 1. The factor-of-~2.4 error (mean household size) makes the lose condition unimplementable as written. Must state: exit books `S_r × household_size` (persons) or redefine the threshold in households.
- **B6 — Death and retirement rates.** Appendix §3 / coupling §1.5, §4.4 / cut list. Age/retire/die is locked in-scope demography, but no doc gives a retirement age/rule, a mortality rate, or initial age distribution. The M0 1-year run's sample shrinkage (coupling open ambiguity 6) and `RETIRE`/`DIE` booking rates are therefore uncodable; item 5's "age/retire/die" third is missing.
- **B7 — Beat-to-need restore mapping.** Appendix §3 + coupling §2.3. Needs decay per game-day and restores ("sleep +60", "home meal +35") are given, but M0 ticks *five sub-daily beats* (time-scale) while rates are per-day: no doc maps which beat applies which restore, in what order, or how the weekly payday / continuous CoL drain interleaves with daily beats. The implementer must invent the tick structure item 5 runs on.
- **B8 — Wage-formula double-count.** Coupling §2.1/§2.2. `W(c,s)` starts at 100 everywhere with "starting differences from sector multipliers," then `offer = W(c,s) × sector_mult × (1+d_f)` multiplies the sector multiplier *again*. Either `W` stores the multiplier (then the offer formula double-counts) or it doesn't (then §2.1's sentence is false). One-line fix needed; until then every paycheck in items 3–5 is wrong by up to 25%.
- **B9 — Commute input and assignment gaps.** Coupling §3.2. Four sub-gaps, all blocking item 6: (a) citizens' `home_xy` and firms' `firm_xy` town assignment (which of the 30 geo towns? by what distribution?); (b) `TRANSIT` availability flag — no appendix policy row creates "Detroit regional transit funding active," so the mode can never turn on; (c) county- vs region-level `roads_c` input (coupling open ambiguity 10 — items 1 and 6 must agree); (d) `season_mult` kill-switch vs cut list (time-scale recommends visual-only default; coupling defaults 1.2× winter with a kill-switch — the default value going into code is undecided).
- **B10 — Policy count and enactment mechanics.** Appendix §2 / roadmap item 7 / GDD §21. Three sub-gaps: (a) 12 appendix rows vs "10 policies" in roadmap item 7 and GDD §21 — the M0 10 (or "bless all 12") is unpicked (coupling open ambiguity 14); (b) cost timing — annual costs deducted when (enact day? quarterly? yearly?), one-time costs (policy 4) vs recurring, what happens on repeal mid-delay; (c) effect application — "+6/yr" style magnitudes applied how within a quarter (linear per-diem? lump at full-effect date?), and approval effects to *which* geography (statewide faction meter vs county). Item 7 cannot be coded.
- **B11 — RNG scheme and save format.** Coupling §1.1/§1.5 + roadmap item 8. Deterministic *ordering* is specified, but the RNG algorithm, seed management (per-tick stream discipline: who consumes randomness in what order), and the save format (what bytes constitute "full sim state," accumulator/counter preservation across save/load per coupling open ambiguity 15) are absent. Item 8's exit test (identical state hash) needs a canonical hash definition too.
- **B12 — Headless tick budget in milliseconds.** Perf-bar §1 + time-scale + roadmap item 9. The ≥2x headroom rule is stated against "the tick's wall-clock budget," which derives from the 45-second day — but the M0 harness is headless and CPU-bound (runs as fast as possible). The pass/fail number in sim-ms-per-tick at 5k/500 and 20k/3k is never computed. (Hardware naming, LANG Q3, compounds it.) Item 9 can be *built* but not *passed*.
- **B13 — Faction-share contradiction (appendix vs north-fix vs legislature).** Appendix §6 (Lakeshore 11 / Yoopers 10) vs north-fix Part A checklist (re-split ~14/~7 under recommended option (a)) vs legislature B1 exact weights (44.3/16.2/10.8/9.9/9.9/9.0, computed from the *old* split). Under option (a) all three cannot be true simultaneously; election blending, sampling audits, and driver weights each cite a different denominator. Lock must recompute and re-stamp all three consistently.
- **B14 — In-state destination choice.** Coupling §4.2 + open ambiguity 2 (review §2.7). The booking identity exists but *where the truck goes* has no rule — not even the promised null model. Appendix §3's move trigger ("2+ needs <15 for 60 days") fires into a void: item 5 produces movers with no destination function.
- **B15 — Interstate destination split / Ohio fraction.** Appendix §5 + coupling open ambiguity 3 + GDD §12. The "Ohio gap" ticker (visible from day one) requires an Ohio share of out-migration; no doc gives one. M0 can book undifferentiated exits, but the ticker — a Phase 2 exit-visible feature fed by the M0 ledger — needs the fraction at lock or the ledger schema ships without the field.

## 3. Resolution proposals (BLOCKING items only; proposals, not facts)

- **B1:** Adopt: Southeast aggregate population = 5.0M (half of 10M); county populations by real census shares (Lucas to confirm table); per-county `roads_c` init 45, per-county unemployment init 5.0%, per-county wage index 100. Appendix v0.2 adds a county table; item 1 builds region→county→sector from it.
- **B2:** Adopt: each tax base initialized from its ±1pt yield (e.g., income base = $2.8B/1pt = $280B taxable base); rate-change revenue follows the appendix schedule as quarterly step (50% Q+1, 100% Q+2+); base/migration elasticity applies as a linear ramp over 6 quarters (midpoint of 4–8). Removes the functional-form invention.
- **B3:** Adopt coupling §1.6 bands into appendix v0.2 *plus* the minimal margin function: `margin = (revenue − wage_bill − tax_cost) / revenue − logistics_penalty`, with `logistics_penalty = logistics_factor − 1` (coupling §3.2) and demand/policy as revenue multipliers from authored event/policy rows. If Lucas dislikes it, he replaces the formula — but *a* formula must be stamped.
- **B4:** Adopt: spawn households by size distribution {1:30%, 2:35%, 3:20%, 4:15%} (mean ≈2.2, close to coupling's 2.4); earners = non-retired adults, one earner flagged primary; starting savings = 2 months CoL per capita; payday = every 7th day from spawn; initial employment 95% of earners assigned to a same-county firm roster, wage = §2 offer formula. (Numbers are proposals; structure is the requirement.)
- **B5:** Adopt: interstate exits/entries book persons: `ledger_delta = ±S_r × household_size`. Threshold stays 250,000 persons. One-line question for Lucas only if he wants households instead: *"Should the exodus threshold count households or persons?"*
- **B6:** Adopt: retirement at age 65 (all 65+ spawn retired; workers retire on birthday); mortality 1.0%/yr flat (~0.0027%/day, age-independent for M0); initial ages uniform 22–64 workers, 65–85 retirees at 15% share. M0 reports opening/closing lens count per coupling open ambiguity 6.
- **B7:** Adopt: daily decays apply evenly across the 5 beats (each beat applies 1/5 of the daily rate); beat restores apply at their beat (sleep→Rest+60 prorated to the sleep beat only, capped 100; meals at home/leisure beats; payday adds cash at the work beat of payday); CoL drains continuously (1/5 per beat per capita). Weekly/monthly timers (30/60-day) evaluate on the daily tick.
- **B8:** Adopt: `W(c,s)` stores the county component only (init 100); `offer = W(c,s) × sector_mult × (1+d_f)`. Delete the §2.1 sentence implying multipliers are baked into W. No number changes; single reading fixed.
- **B9:** Adopt: (a) homes/firms assigned to towns by county population weight (geo towns mapped to counties; Lucas confirms mapping); (b) TRANSIT available iff a "Detroit regional transit funded" flag is set — flag defaults OFF, set by a new appendix policy row 13 or by demand/event id (Lucas picks which); (c) `roads_c` is county-level, region mean as fallback when a county value is missing; (d) `season_mult` defaults 1.0 (visual-only lock per time-scale recommendation) with the formula retained behind the flag.
- **B10:** Adopt: M0 ships appendix policies 1–10 (drop 11 PFAS and 12 shoreline — the two Lakeshore-pure rows least relevant to an SE slice; keeps the count the roadmap/GDD promise); annual costs deducted quarterly (1/4 per quarter from enact quarter); one-time costs on enact day; repeal stops future deductions, effects decay over their stated full-effect delay; approval effects accrue linearly per quarter to the statewide faction meter. Lucas may swap which two are dropped.
- **B11:** Adopt: RNG = SplitMix64/FNV-1a-style seeded stream, single global sequence consumed in `(day, firm_id, citizen_id)` order with per-system substream derivation documented in code; save format = full struct dump incl. accumulator states, RNG counter, and next `event_id` counter; state hash = FNV-1a over the canonical serialization (matching the LANG spike's checksum precedent). Exact algorithm stamped in the Phase 1 item 8 task order.
- **B12:** Adopt: compute the headless pass numbers from the time-scale cadence at 2x cruise: one game-day = 22.5 wall-clock seconds ⇒ full-tick (daily macro + 5 beats + placement + logging for 20k/3k) must complete in ≤11.25s (50%) — and record a *stretch* target of ≤100ms as the engineering bar that proves decoupling headroom (coupling §5 predicts single-digit ms). If that pair looks wrong, Lucas sets the ms number before item 9 starts.
- **B13:** Adopt north-fix option (a) with Lakeshore 14 / Yoopers 7 (total still 100), and recompute legislature B1 weights from the new base shares at lock (mechanism ×1.3 unchanged). Restamp appendix §6, legislature B1, coupling §4.4, and attribution §6-M1-subset in one edit pass.
- **B14:** Adopt null model: destination = weighted-random county by posted openings (coupling's own candidate), ties to same-region, never out-of-state (interstate is the separate §3 trigger). One sentence in appendix v0.2.
- **B15:** Adopt: Ohio share = 35% of net interstate outflow (proposal; the gag needs Ohio plurality, not majority). Ledger schema carries `destination ∈ {Ohio, other}` from M0 even though M0 books undifferentiated totals, so Phase 2's ticker needs no migration.

## 4. Verdict

**No — Phase 0 is not done on paper: 15 blocking ambiguities (missing profit function, county disaggregation, tax-base mechanics, household initialization, ledger units, vital rates, tick mapping, wage double-count, commute inputs, policy count/enactment, RNG/save format, headless ms budget, faction-share contradiction, and both destination rules) must be resolved before Phase 1 starts.**

Highest-stakes confirmations: the Money-vs-household rule is structurally sound but uninitializable (B4) and its exodus ledger miscounts persons as households (B5); the wage rule is code-shaped except for a sector-multiplier double-count (B8) and invented dollar mappings living only in a proposal doc; the scaling law is coherent but its Southeast population constant and person-counting are unstamped (B1, B5); the UP small-sample rule is well-designed yet parameterized against shares that the north-fix decision invalidates (B13, S4); and the commute abstraction is the most honestly specified+signed-off pick in Phase 0 but cannot run without town assignment and a TRANSIT-activation source (B9).
